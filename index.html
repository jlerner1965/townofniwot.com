Neutral election guide

Niwot incorporation

Registered electors inside the proposed boundary will vote on incorporation, a home rule charter commission, its members, and five fiscal issues. This guide identifies sources by role and does not take a position.

Current deadline Written comments for or against the five fiscal issues are due September 18, 2026 at noon. How to file ↗
3

Ballot questions

Incorporation, formation of a nine-member home rule charter commission, and election of its members.

5

Fiscal issues

Sales and use tax, property tax, marijuana tax, revenue retention, and transportation-related debt authorization.

Niwot’s story

A place shaped by deeper histories.

Niwot is named for Chief Niwot, an Arapaho leader whose people lived along Colorado’s Front Range. The community later grew around agriculture, the railroad, and the commercial district that remains central to local life.
